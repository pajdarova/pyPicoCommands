Built 2026-11-02 from `picoscope_sdk.py` (same commands as Picoscope_5000D / Picoscope_pyPicoSDK, which stay unchanged until the common module matches them on the 5443D). Department instruments: 4x 2408B (ps2000a), 2x 5443D (ps5000a), 1x 4824A (ps4000a), plus a 3000-series scope with the deposition device (model unknown; ps3000a if 3000A/B/D, a 3000E would need psospa = not covered).

Design decisions:
- Own ctypes against the driver DLLs (no pyPicoSDK, no picosdk wrappers); prototypes taken from the PicoSDK headers in `C:\Program Files\Pico Technology\SDK\inc`. One `_HwBackend(family)` driven by a `_Family` table (oversample argument, trigger API v1/arr/v2, selectable resolution, extras only in ps5000a, EXT code 4 vs 8). `PICO_SDK_DIR` overrides the DLL folder.
- OPEN enumerates the drivers (ps5000a, ps4000a, ps3000a, ps2000a) with `EnumerateUnits`; `OPEN <serial>`, `OPEN PS3000A [serial]`, `OPEN SIM [variant]` (sim models 5443D, 2408B, 2204A, 3404D MSO, 3206D, 4824A), `DEVICES?`, `CAPS?`. VARIANT second digit = number of channels, "MSO" = digital inputs.
- "Not supported": static checks for what the API lacks (resolution only ps5000a, pulse width not ps4000a, WITHINPRE/EDGEDETECT only ps5000a, digital only MSO, trigger info only ps3000a/ps5000a, missing driver functions) plus driver statuses (INVALID_VOLTAGE_RANGE, NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_THIS_DEVICE, ...) mapped to `ExceptionNotSupported`. `strict=False` gives `ERR <cmd>: Not supported: ...`.
- Fixed-resolution models: `OSC BITS` accepts only the fixed value (8 bit ps2000a/3000a, 12 bit ps4000a); default follows the model at OPEN.
- Auto trigger in ms on ps2000a/3000a/4000a (rounded up), microseconds on ps5000a.
- 8-channel scopes (4824A): channels A-H. The generator command is `GEN` in all three PicoScope modules (the old short `G` was removed 2026-11-02, it clashed with channel G); `G` is only channel G.
- `X FACTOR`, `OSC RESET` as in the other modules.
- Models without a generator: OPEN's 0 V attempt is swallowed, `CAPS?` shows GENERATOR=OFF, `GEN ON` gives Not supported.

Trigger position and delay (5443D, generator on D and EXT, 2026-11-02): time axis verified. With PRETRIGGER p and TRG DELAY n (samples) the trigger edge sits at sample p-n and get_time() puts it at t = -(half sample) (trigger sample = first sample above the level); a 1 kHz square's next edges land exactly where p-n+100 samples predicts, for n = 0..1000 and for single block and rapid block, with D or EXT as trigger source. get_trigger_times() gives the sub-sample offset for analog sources (5 us = half a sample for a fast edge at 10 us/sample; 0 or 500 ns scatter at 1 us) but always 0 on EXT (EXT jitters by one sample). The driver's triggerIndex (get_trigger_info) is not exactly p-n with a delay (p-n-2 at n=30, p-n-4 at n=60): the module does not use it for the time axis.
EXT trigger: RISING/FALLING/BOTH work at all levels; ABOVE/BELOW fire at ANY level on EXT (work correctly on A-D) -> module now refuses them on EXT (TRG EDGE EXT ABOVE / TRG EXT DIR ABOVE|BELOW|window) in all three PicoScope modules. EXT auto hysteresis is 1 % of 5 V = 50 mV: a 20 mVpp signal does not trigger without TRG EXT HYST 5m. Generator max is 4 Vpp (status 0x39 PICO_SIGGEN_PK_TO_PK).

Command groups renamed 2026-11-02 in all three PicoScope modules (Andrea's decision, for consistency and because single letters clash with channels A-H): acquisition `O` -> `OSC`, trigger `T` -> `TRG`, generator `G` -> `GEN`; the old short forms are removed (O and T give "unknown command", G is only channel G). Examples: `OSC INTERVAL 10us; TRG EDGE A RISING 0.5; GEN ON`, `OSC?`, `TRG?`, `GEN?`. Older notes in memory/ were rewritten to the new names. ES_CRDS had no calls to these modules yet, so nothing else had to change.

Bugs found and fixed (all three modules): (1) STOP race - WAIT reported ABORTED before ps5000aStop finished, state still ARMED and the next command failed ("capture is running" / PICO_DRIVER_FUNCTION 0x43); now `_stop_idle` event: WAIT and every command wait for a STOP in progress. (2) After a window trigger the driver refused every later level trigger (PICO_INVALID_TRIGGER_PROPERTY): directions must be sent BEFORE the channel properties. (3) Trigger level beyond the range after adding the analog offset gave the cryptic PICO_THRESHOLD_OUT_OF_RANGE; now a clear "trigger level ... is outside the input range". (4) ETS on a channel other than A fails at RunBlock with PICO_TRIGGER_ERROR (0x2C): the modules now say "ETS works with channel A only".

## EXT trigger on the 5443D (hardware, generator on D and EXT)

- RISING / FALLING fire on the matching edge at record index 99.5; with sine, the level is honoured: D at the trigger point equals the EXT level (-0.4 ... +0.4 V, error < 15 mV) and the slope sign matches the direction.
- Levels outside +-5 V are refused with the range message. AND / OR of D and EXT behave as expected (6/6). TRG DELAY 30 shifts the edge to 69.5. 20/20 repeated shots stable.
- Levels just outside the signal swing (square +-0.5 V, level +-0.6 V) fire only sporadically (comparator hysteresis / overshoot); levels well outside (+-2, +-4 V) never fire for RISING / FALLING.
- BOTH ignores the level: it fires on every edge even at +-2 V (same quirk family as ABOVE/BELOW, which are refused). Use RISING or FALLING when the level matters. Not yet restricted in the code.

## ETS on the 5443D (hardware, generator on A, 5 MHz sine, 8 ns interval)

- BUG fixed in all three modules: `GetValues` in ETS mode returns the requested number of samples (500), not `interleave` times more; asking for more gave PICO_TOO_MANY_SAMPLES. The time axis comes from `SetEtsTimeBuffer` (step = interval / interleave, slightly irregular).
- `SetEts` accepts cycles >= interleave (cycles 4 / interleave 20 and 1/10 give PICO_INVALID_PARAMETER).
- Verified: FAST and SLOW, interleave 2/4/10/20 -> steps 4/2/0.8/0.4 ns; fitted amplitude 0.498-0.500 V (expected 0.500), fit residual 3-4 mV, same as normal block; works with auto trigger; second channel refused with the channel-A message.
